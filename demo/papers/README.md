# Demo papers

Two published papers and what the MIRA extraction app made from them. They give the demo real material with no setup.

| Paper | Graph | Units | Relations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Predicting eventual development of lupus nephritis at the time of diagnosis of systemic lupus erythematosus | `lupus-nephritis-prediction.graph.json` | 30 | 51 |
| Dysregulation of innate and adaptive serum mediators precedes systemic lupus erythematosus classification | `preclinical-sle-mediators.graph.json` | 25 | 29 |

The `.mira.jsonld` files are the same graphs in MIRA's linked data form.

The PDFs belong to their publishers, and they're here so people can run the demo.
