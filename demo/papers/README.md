# Demo papers

Two published papers and the graph the [MIRA extraction app](https://mira-extraction.vercel.app/) made from each. **Load demo papers** in the Library, and the **Demo replay** model in the upload dialog, use them, so the demo has real material with no server and no model.

| Paper | PDF | Graph | Units | Relations |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Predicting eventual development of lupus nephritis at the time of diagnosis of systemic lupus erythematosus | `lupus-nephritis-prediction.pdf` | `lupus-nephritis-prediction.graph.json` | 30 | 51 |
| Dysregulation of innate and adaptive serum mediators precedes systemic lupus erythematosus classification | `preclinical-sle-mediators.pdf` | `preclinical-sle-mediators.graph.json` | 25 | 29 |

The `.mira.jsonld` files are the same graphs in MIRA's linked data form.

The PDFs belong to their publishers. They are here so people can run the demo.
